Company Policies & BAU Internship Framework
Al-Balqa Applied University Accreditation

All host organizations agree to provide structured mentorship for at least 280 logged training hours across an 8-week period.

1. Mentorship & Workspace Safety

Host companies assign a designated senior engineer/mentor who conducts weekly check-ins and signs off on weekly training logbooks.

2. Attendance & Leave Protocols

Students must maintain 90%+ attendance. Any absence exceeding 2 days requires faculty coordinator approval and medical proof.

3. Evaluation & Rubric Delivery

The 8-criterion final evaluation matrix must be filled and digitally signed through this portal upon completion of the training period.
